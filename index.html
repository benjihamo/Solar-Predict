2 · Battery & electricity rates

We compare tomorrow’s solar with your typical daily home use, then estimate how much energy to add overnight.

Enter your typical total household use per day. It helps estimate how much solar may remain to charge the battery. Import battery-flow history below to estimate bedtime charge and overnight use. This is a guide, not hourly modelling or battery control.

Battery-flow history Any system

Use an open CSV from your battery monitor. Sunwise learns a typical bedtime charge and overnight battery use. Your readings stay on this device.

Required CSV columns: timestamp (ISO date/time), soc_percent (0–100), and battery_discharge_w (battery output in watts). Include readings near 22:00 and regularly through 06:00. Other brands can use this same format.

3 · Your location

Your coordinates are saved on this device. Each forecast request sends them to Open-Meteo so it can return local sunlight data. Device location is optional; you can enter coordinates yourself.
