Privacy policy

For Sunwise · Solar Predict. Effective 29 September 2026.

This policy describes the Sunwise solar forecast app and its Android wrapper. The app has no user accounts, advertising, or analytics. Solar Predict is maintained in the public project repository.

Information on your device

Your panel details, battery and tariff settings, coordinates, and solar readings you enter for calibration are saved in local browser storage on your device. They are not uploaded to a Solar Predict account or synced between devices. The Android app has its own local storage, separate from the browser version. Clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or clear this site’s browser storage, to remove the saved settings and history.

Location and forecast requests

Device location is optional. You can type in coordinates instead. If you choose “Use my device location,” the Android app asks for approximate location permission after explaining why. The location is used to fill in your coordinates. Those coordinates are stored on your device with your setup and sent to Open-Meteo with each forecast request so it can return local solar weather data. The request also necessarily includes your network IP address and the requested URL.

Open-Meteo states that its API logs may include IP addresses and requested URLs, and that troubleshooting logs may include geographical coordinates. Its published terms say individual log files are removed after 90 days. Read the Open-Meteo privacy terms for its current practices.

Hosting

The app and this policy are hosted by GitHub Pages. When your device loads the site, GitHub processes website usage and technical data under its General Privacy Statement. Forecast requests go directly from the app to Open-Meteo; Solar Predict does not operate a server that receives or stores those requests.

Security and retention

Connections to the app, GitHub Pages, and the forecast API use HTTPS. Settings and calibration history remain on your device until you clear them or remove the app. Solar Predict does not keep a copy. Open-Meteo and GitHub handle any request logs under their own published retention practices.

Privacy questions

You can contact the developer through the Solar Predict project issues page. That page is public, so do not include private information in an issue.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated here. Check the effective date at the top of this page.
